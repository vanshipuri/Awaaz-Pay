    HOW IT WORKS

    A safer way to say “pay”

    AwaazPay does not blindly press Pay. It translates your words into a payment intent, checks that intent against your saved payees, spending patterns, and caregiver mandate, and speaks the decision back to you.

    1Say it naturallyHindi, English, or Hinglish phrases are welcome.
    2Hear the truthAmount, payee, direction, and risk are spoken plainly.
    3Choose deliberatelyNo spoken yes means no payment.
    4Say your Voice PINThe last step replaces the visual UPI PIN pad and releases the hands-free mandate charge.
